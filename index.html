LoadsOfMaths Games

Work-in-progress classroom games. Tap a title to play.

  • 24 Game
    Combine four numbers to make 24.
  • Angle Detective
    Hunt for the missing angle and choose the correct reason, from straight lines and vertically opposite angles to parallel lines and multi-step GCSE problems. Nine levels, with an option for angles in multiples of 5°.
  • Baked Bean Sim
    GCSE Statistics distribution explorer: watch normal, geometric, binomial and Poisson distributions build up from simulated data, and run a baked bean factory for profit.
  • Block Shift
    Slide the wooden blocks into place, from 2×2 up to 5×5.
  • Bouncer
    Let in only the numbers that fit the rule, then add up who got in.
  • Brain Gym
    Chains of operations to work through.
  • Can You Predict the Future?
    A funfair crystal ball tests your psychic powers with coin tosses, red or black cards, ESP cards, dice, the lottery, a cups-and-ball game and a prize wheel. Is the score impressive, or just luck? A whole-class hands-up mode and tally for discussing significance.
  • Chocolate Biscuits
    A match-three game with a sliding pile: match three chocolate biscuits to collapse the whole pile and move up a level, but the pile creeps down faster every time. If it reaches the floor, answer a fractions question against the clock for a lifeline.
  • Count Protracula's Coffin Quiz
    Mixed maths with a vampire quizmaster: open the right coffin, then bank your pumpkins or risk them.
  • Dance Floor Filler
    Rotate each tile and fit it onto the 5×5 grid before the clock runs out. Climb from placing the last three tiles to a completely blank grid, then watch the tiles dance. Six party music styles.
  • Data Lab
    GCSE Statistics workbench: generate data, then build tally charts, frequency tables, histograms, cumulative frequency curves, box plots, stem and leaf diagrams and scatter graphs.
  • Dave
    The sequence memory game: repeat the growing pattern of lights and notes, alone or taking turns to add a new colour of your own.
  • Dice Games
    Pig, Snake Eyes, 10,000, Ship, Captain & Crew, Shut The Box and Yacht: roll, risk it or bank your points.
  • Erno
    Restore a scrambled coloured grid using rotations and reflections.
  • Factris
    Divide to survive! Bricks fall onto a growing wall: divide the wall by the brick when you can, and build it into numbers with plenty of factors so the next bricks fit. Twenty levels, fading hints, and a final wall to clear.
  • Flip!
    A two-player strategy game on a 7×7 board of two-sided tiles: place line, diagonal and circle tokens to flip tiles from orange to blue and back again, and finish with the most. Play a friend or the computer at three levels, with undo and a move list.
  • Fractal Explorer
    Zoom into the Mandelbrot set, play the chaos game, grow a fern or a tree, and watch the Koch, Sierpinski, Jurassic dragon, Lévy, Hilbert and Peano curves build up generation by generation. Save any picture, even at high resolution.
  • Fraction Blaster
    Retro wireframe asteroids with a protractor laser: all but one of the fractions are equivalent, so set the angle and blast the imposter. One or two players, with levels, lives and 8-bit music.
  • Fraction Fight Night
    Compare fractions in the boxing ring.
  • Goods Inwards
    A metric conversions starter set in a workshop stores: check each goods label against the order form, converting lengths, masses, capacities and more. Four levels, from simple ×10 and ×1000 up to areas, with an optional countdown.
  • Hanoi
    The classic Tower of Hanoi puzzle: move the stack to the goal pillar in as few moves as you can, with more disks at every level.
  • Heads or Tails
    Flip the coin: a class knockout game, plus an investigation into runs and patterns.
  • Higher or Lower
    Will the next card be higher or lower?
  • Jelly Bean Disco
    Times tables on the dance floor: pop the jelly bean with the right answer.
  • Keep Left
    Keep it or skip it? Sort the number plates against the clock, from even numbers to primes above 100 and multiples of 17. Every wrong card adds time, and a Highway Code question at the end is worth ±5 seconds. Five licence levels, from Learner Driver to Master Driver.
  • Loads of Frames
    Perimeter and area, as a picture frame seller.
  • Lucky Duck
    Estimation: pick the duck closest to the answer.
  • Maze Munch Maths
    A maze chase with a maths twist: munch dots for +2, grab ×2, ×5 and ×10 to multiply your score, and dodge the ×0 monsters. Five skill levels, plus a joystick pad for the IWB and phones.
  • Not There
    The high-stakes arithmetic race: pick A, B, C or D, or “Not there”. Right doubles your score, wrong quarters it. Only for the brave!
  • Nul Points!
    The Eurovision-style maths game where zero is the best score: pick a correct answer that few others would think of.
  • Number Forensics
    Find the imposter number, or the imposter fraction.
  • Number Memory Game
    Remember and recall the sequence.
  • Over & Under
    A flappy-owl number game: fly over one type of number and under the other – primes and composites, fractions and a half, squares, negatives and more. Starts with a single number and builds up to a stack of three.
  • Pancake Flipper
    A Puzzle Game based on the Pancake Sort, with a Sort Explorer tab to investigate some other known sorting methods.
  • Penguins & Icebergs
    Directed numbers practice.
  • Pentomino Explorer
    A calm, late-night pentomino playground: fill an outline with the twelve tiles (rectangles, free-form shapes or giant tiles), or just make pictures. Optional lounge music with a mixer.
  • Potato
    What comes next in the sequence?
  • Prime Quest
    A two-team prime-hunting game on a coordinate grid: flip tiles, collect primes, bank your points before you bust. First to 531 wins.
  • Recite Pi
    How many digits of pi can you recite? Beat your personal best.
  • Sports Day
    A phone stopwatch for race day: tap each runner as they cross the line to record lap times, speeds and best laps.
  • Super Fun Maths Quiz
    A quick-fire mixed-topic starter quiz: 10 or 20 questions that move on by themselves, with diagrams and optional multiple choice.
  • Tangram Explorer
    A calm, late-night tangram playground: fill a silhouette with the seven pieces, or just make pictures. Coloured or solid black pieces, sharp or rounded corners, and optional lounge music.
  • The Choice
    Blind auditions for maths detectives: clues appear one by one, buzz to spin your chair, then choose the mystery number, 2D shape or 3D solid from four cards.
  • Times Tables Quiz
    Times tables practice.
  • Times Tables Tile Match
    Tap a calculation and the tile that makes the same number. Choose your tables, then mix times tables, division and factor pairs (2×10 with 4×5). Twenty pairs a round, harder facts score more, with a list of facts to practise at the end. Solo with a time limit, or two teams taking turns.
  • Toast
    A gentle timing game: eject the toast just before it pops. How good is your sense of time?
  • Triangle Tangle
    Solve triangles step by step, from angle sums and Pythagoras to sine and cosine rules and 3-D triangles. Twenty levels with worked solutions.
  • Trio
    Spot the sets of cards that go together.
  • Whack-A-Prime
    A garden whack-a-mole for primes: tap the moles holding prime numbers and they cheer, tap a non-prime and the mole gets sad. Choose the number range, speed and holes, and see the factors when you slip up.
  • Whale Watching
    Solve linear equations from a boat by balancing both sides: throw tiles at the equation and keep your distance from the whales. Free play or a 10 or 20 question quiz.
  • Wildlife Line-Up
    Line up the decimal points, sort animal measurements and find the median.
