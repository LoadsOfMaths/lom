LoadsOfMaths Games

Work-in-progress classroom games. Tap a title to play.

  • 24 Game
    Combine four numbers to make 24.
  • Baked Bean Sim
    GCSE Statistics distribution explorer: watch normal, geometric, binomial and Poisson distributions build up from simulated data, and run a baked bean factory for profit.
  • Block Shift
    Slide the wooden blocks into place, from 2×2 up to 5×5.
  • Bouncer
    Let in only the numbers that fit the rule, then add up who got in.
  • Brain Gym
    Chains of operations to work through.
  • Count Protracula's Coffin Quiz
    Mixed maths with a vampire quizmaster: open the right coffin, then bank your pumpkins or risk them.
  • Data Lab
    GCSE Statistics workbench: generate data, then build tally charts, frequency tables, histograms, cumulative frequency curves, box plots, stem and leaf diagrams and scatter graphs.
  • Dice Games
    Pig, Snake Eyes and 10,000: roll, risk it or bank your points.
  • Erno
    Restore a scrambled coloured grid using rotations and reflections.
  • Fraction Fight Night
    Compare fractions in the boxing ring.
  • Heads or Tails
    Flip the coin: a class knockout game, plus an investigation into runs and patterns.
  • Higher or Lower
    Will the next card be higher or lower?
  • Jelly Bean Disco
    Times tables on the dance floor: pop the jelly bean with the right answer.
  • Loads of Frames
    Perimeter and area, as a picture frame seller.
  • Lucky Duck
    Estimation: pick the duck closest to the answer.
  • Not There
    The high-stakes arithmetic race: pick A, B, C or D, or “Not there”. Right doubles your score, wrong quarters it. Only for the brave!
  • Nul Points!
    The Eurovision-style maths game where zero is the best score: pick a correct answer that few others would think of.
  • Number Forensics
    Find the imposter number.
  • Number Memory Game
    Remember and recall the sequence.
  • Penguins & Icebergs
    Directed numbers practice.
  • Potato
    What comes next in the sequence?
  • Recite Pi
    How many digits of pi can you recite? Beat your personal best.
  • Sports Day
    A phone stopwatch for race day: tap each runner as they cross the line to record lap times, speeds and best laps.
  • Super Fun Maths Quiz
    A quick-fire mixed-topic starter quiz: 10 or 20 questions that move on by themselves, with diagrams and optional multiple choice.
  • The Choice
    Blind auditions for number detectives: clues appear one by one, buzz to spin your chair, then choose the mystery number from four cards.
  • Times Tables Quiz
    Times tables practice.
  • Toast
    A gentle timing game: eject the toast just before it pops. How good is your sense of time?
  • Trio
    Spot the sets of cards that go together.
  • Whale Watching
    Solve linear equations from a boat by balancing both sides: throw tiles at the equation and keep your distance from the whales. Free play or a 10 or 20 question quiz.
  • Wildlife Line-Up
    Line up the decimal points, sort animal measurements and find the median.
Draft versions for testing. Updates are published here as they are finished.
