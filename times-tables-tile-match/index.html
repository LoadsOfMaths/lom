Times Tables Tile Match

1Which times tables?

The presets just tick a set of tables for you – add or remove any you like afterwards. Every tile uses one of your ticked tables with a number from 2 to 12.

2What sort of matches?

Switch on more than one to mix them. With all three on, any two tiles that make the same number can be matched – so 56÷7 could go with 8 or with 2×4.

3Who is playing?

A round is 20 pairs. Each pair scores the number it makes, so the harder facts are worth more. Tiles you have tapped never change. Teams take turns, one go each (right or wrong); the time limit is for solo play.

Tap a tile, then tap a tile that makes the same number. A wrong pair costs you nothing but your streak. Best scores are saved in this browser, on this computer only – nothing is sent anywhere.
