How to play Hot Seat!

Sit or Stand – Everyone decides to sit or stand. Each time a row or column is called, everyone in it swaps: sitters stand, standers sit. After at least five calls the computer announces whether the winners are seated or standing.

Last Ones Standing – Everyone stands. When your row or column is called, sit down – you are out. Anyone still standing when the calls stop is a winner.

Work It Out – You see the whole sequence and whether the winners will be seated or standing before it starts. Count how many times your row and your column are called, and choose where to start. Watch out: the sequence may disappear, and there may be a time limit!

The maths – Only the total number of times your row and column are called matters. Even: you finish where you started. Odd: you finish the other way round.

Keyboard: Space or Enter presses the big button.
