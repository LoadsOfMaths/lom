How to Play Yoikes!

  • The teacher calls out random numbers from 1–100.
  • Each student has a row of boxes in the back of their book, plus a Yoikes! Bin.
  • As each number is called, write it into a box so that the numbers increase from left to right along the row.
  • If a number can’t be placed without breaking that ascending order, shout “Yoikes!” and put it in the bin instead.
  • The winner has the most numbers successfully boxed at the end — 8 or 9 usually wins, and all 10 is rare!
  • Several students can win at once, so everyone stays in with a chance.

Variants
Standard – 10 boxes. Royal – 20 boxes. Gambler – 2 boxes, and the first number called is always 50.

Game modes
Manual – you call the numbers with a button for a class playing on paper.
Live play – play on screen, clicking a box or “Yoikes!” for each number.
Auto – watch a simple computer player place its numbers, then see if students can beat it.
