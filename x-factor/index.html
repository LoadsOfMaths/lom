X-FACTOR

You are the judge. Does the act have what it takes?

Each act is a mystery number. Its Factor Facts arrive one card at a time, starting with two. After each card, make your call:

  • Green for Go: "Next card, please." More than one number still fits.
  • Golden Buzzer: "I know the number!" The facts so far fit exactly one number.
  • Red Buzzer: "Imposter!" The facts clash, so no number can fit them all.

Scoring: +1 for a correct buzz, −1 for a wrong buzz. If you ask for another card when the act was already decided, you were too slow and score 0. Keyboard: 1 Green, 2 Golden, 3 Red, Enter for the next act.
