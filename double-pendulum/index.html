Double Pendulum

Two pendulums joined end to end. The rules are simple, yet the motion is chaotic: a tiny change at the start gives a completely different swing later. Drag a bob sideways and let go.

Start from

Drag either bob to a new position, then let go. The pendulum swings until friction brings it to rest, or you can grab a bob to stop it. Press the space bar to pause.
Questions to explore
  • Give it a small swing. Does the motion look regular? Now drag the lower bob right up high before letting go. What changes?
  • Turn on the twin and let go from a big angle. How long does it take before the two pendulums look completely different? Try again from a small swing.
  • Set the friction to 0 and let it swing. Does it ever stop? What is happening to the energy?
  • Make the lower mass much bigger than the upper one, then the other way round. Which pendulum is pushed around more?
  • The time for one swing of a single pendulum is T = 2π√(L ÷ g). What happens to T if you make g four times as large?
  • On the Moon, g is about 1.62. Predict how the swing will change, then check.
