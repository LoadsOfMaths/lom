Venn Diagrams

A quiet place to sort numbers into sets.

Three sets are described. Drag each number into the right part of the diagram: in just one circle, in an overlap, or outside all of them. Exactly one number sits in the very middle, in all three sets.

Then read the diagram: find the numbers in A ∩ B (both), A ∪ B (either), A′ (not in A), and count with n(A).
