HOW TO PLAY

  • Click a tile to turn it over and reveal its number.
  • Found a prime? Click it again to collect it for your Turn Total.
  • Click Bank it to add your Turn Total to your Quest Points. That ends your go.
  • Click a non-prime a second time and you bust. So does turning over a multiple of 10 (Easy) or 5 (Hard). Bust and you lose everything in your Turn Total.
  • With Prime Flash on, a prime you do not collect straight away flies off and is lost for the turn.
  • The first team to reach 531 Quest Points wins. There are 530 points on either side of the primes up to 100, so claim a majority!
