NUL POINTS!

The Maths game where zero is the best score.

How to play

  1. A maths question appears. There are lots of correct answers.
  2. Everyone writes one answer on paper or a mini-whiteboard. Nothing is typed into the computer.
  3. The teacher reveals the points board. Each answer has a score – the lower the better.
  4. Find your answer and add its points to your running total.
  5. The Nul Hunt: a correct answer that few people would think of scores NUL POINTS! Don’t just solve the maths – think about which correct answer is least obvious.
  6. On a phone? Choose Solo (phone) in Settings: type your answer, tap reveal, and the app keeps your score for you.
  7. A wrong answer scores a heavy penalty (you choose how heavy in Settings).
  8. Not on the board? Every valid answer still has a score – the teacher uses Check an answer.
  9. After the last round, the lowest total wins.

Settings
