Millionaire

How high can you climb?

You start on 1 point.
Easy questions: a correct answer multiplies your score by 2.
Hard questions: by 5.
A wrong answer just multiplies by 1: you keep what you have. A perfect round makes 1,000,000.

Interactive

Game-show style: tap your answer and watch the score climb. Three lifelines: 50:50, Ask the Class and Ask a Friend.

Class game

Hands-free: each question is on the board for 30 seconds. Students write their answers, then you mark together at the end.
