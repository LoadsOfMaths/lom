Bubble Pop – settings

How to play

The board is a sheet of silicone bubbles, just like the fidget toy. Players take turns to pop bubbles.

  • Tap a bubble to pop it straight away (or drag along a row to pop several). When you have finished your turn, press End turn.
  • If you prefer, switch on Confirm pop in Settings: you pick bubbles first, then press Pop!
  • In One row mode you may pop as many bubbles as you like, but all from the same row.
  • In Anywhere mode you may pop from any rows, but only up to the limit each turn.
  • Depending on the settings, whoever pops the last bubble loses – or wins.

Stuck? The Hint button shows a winning move if there is one.
