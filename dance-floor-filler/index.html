Dance Floor Filler

Fill the whole 5 × 5 grid before the clock runs out on any piece!

  • A tile appears. Tap it (or use the buttons, or press R) to turn it a quarter-turn.
  • Drag it onto the grid and let go to place it. Tiles can be turned but not flipped over.
  • Each tile has its own countdown, and the next tile only arrives once you have placed this one.
  • When climbing, most of the grid is already filled in (the dimmer tiles). Level 1 asks for just the last 3 tiles, each level adds one more, and the final level starts from a blank grid.
  • Cover every square with the last tile and the party starts!
