FACTRIS

DIVIDE TO SURVIVE!

1THE BRICK DIVIDES

A brick marked ÷5 is falling. The sign at the bottom shows the wall. The brick is what you divide the wall by.

2FACTOR!

If the wall is a multiple of the brick, press FACTOR and the wall is divided: 12 ÷ 3 = 4. If the wall equals the brick, you clear it completely for a big bonus. Press it when it doesn't divide and you pay for it: +5.

3BUILD AHEAD!

Every brick still lands and adds its value to the wall: wall 7 + brick 5 = 12. So think ahead. Build walls with lots of factors (12, 24, 36, 60…) and check the next bricks to see what will fit. A wall of 47 is a nightmare; a wall of 12 is a gift.

4DON'T BREACH IT

Reach the top and you lose a heart. Hints fade as you climb the levels.

5WIN

Reach Level 20, then clear the final wall 3 times to finish the build.

Space = FACTOR  ·  ↓ (hold) = drop faster  ·  P = pause  ·  M = music
