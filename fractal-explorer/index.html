Chaos Game

Pick a corner at random. Jump part of the way towards it. Plot a dot. Repeat. Where do the dots end up?

Press Step to take one jump at a time, or Run to let the computer do thousands.
Questions to explore
  • The corners are picked at random, so why does the same picture appear every time?
  • Point P is at (2, 6) and the corner is at (10, 2). Where is the point half way between them? What if the jump fraction is ⅔?
  • Which jump fractions work for a square? What does the “never repeat” rule change?
  • The triangle has dimension log 3 ÷ log 2 ≈ 1.585. What could “between a line and a surface” mean?

Fern

Four rules, each one shrinks, turns and moves the whole picture. A dice-roll picks which rule is used next. Change the numbers to “break” the fern.

Each rule turns a point (x, y) into a new point:
x′ = ax + by + e
y′ = cx + dy + f
p is how likely the rule is to be picked. Untick a rule to switch it off.
Questions to explore
  • Switch the rules off one at a time. Which part of the fern does each one build?
  • Rule 2 keeps 85% of the size. Where is the “whole fern, slightly smaller” hiding inside the fern?
  • The stem rule has a probability of only 1%. What happens if you make it 30%?
  • The triangle needs only three rules, and all of them have a = d = 0.5. Why 0.5?

Questions to explore

    Tree

    Every branch splits into smaller branches. Change the angle and the shrink ratio to grow a different tree.

    Questions to explore
    • Which angle and ratio give the tree that looks most real to you? Does it match a real tree’s shape?
    • With 2 branches each time and ratio 0.6, how many twigs are there after 10 splits? Check the count.
    • Add up the lengths of all the branches. What ratio makes the total stay finite as the splits go on for ever?
    • What does the shape look like when the angle is very small? Very large?
