How to play

Each question shows the start of a sequence. The potato is hiding the next number — work out the rule and write down what comes next. Tap the potato (or press Enter) to reveal it.

Difficulty

Switch on Different rule every question in Settings if you don't want two lots of triangular numbers, or multiples of 7 going up and then going down, in the same set. Mash starts sequences near the beginning (2, 4, 6, 8…). Each level up starts further along — by Oven Baked you'll see things like 41, 43, 47, 53…, bigger multiples, and quadratics with second differences of 1 to 6. Roasties and Oven Baked include negative numbers in add/subtract sequences, and finish with a puzzle question (switch this off in Settings).
