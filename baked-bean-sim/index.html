💷 Costs and prices

A tin costs two-thirds beans (so more beans = more cost) and one-third tin, label and packing. A standard machine holds a tolerance of σ = 5 g; anything tighter costs premium × (5 ÷ σ − 1) pence extra per tin. Underweight tins are sold but must be compensated. Tins fuller than the burst limit burst: no sale, plus clean-up. At 50p about 10,000 tins a day sell; a higher sensitivity means customers desert you faster as the price rises.
